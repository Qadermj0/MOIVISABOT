# Kuwait Visa AI Assistant

## Project Overview

Kuwait Visa AI Assistant is a smart conversational assistant designed to help users understand Kuwait visa options and basic eligibility information in a simple, friendly way.

The assistant allows users to ask natural questions about available visa types, age requirements, allowed occupations, companion or relationship rules, and general visa details based on the applicant's nationality.

The goal of the project is to make visa information easier to access, especially for users who may ask questions in different ways, languages, or informal wording.

## What The Assistant Helps With

- Finding available Kuwait visa types for a specific nationality.
- Explaining basic details for a selected visa.
- Checking whether an applicant may be eligible based on available rules.
- Providing a direct eligibility check through the **Start Eligibility Check** button.
- Answering questions about allowed occupations.
- Answering questions about allowed relationships or companions.
- Supporting natural conversation in Arabic and English.

## Direct Eligibility Check

The project also includes a direct eligibility check option through the **Start Eligibility Check** button.

This feature allows users to select or enter the required applicant details in a guided form instead of asking through chat. It is useful when the user wants a quick structured check based on nationality, visa type, age, occupation, gender, and relationship or companion details when needed.

The result gives the user a clear preliminary status and highlights what information matched, what did not match, or what details are still missing.

## Example Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Available Visas

User: What visas are available for Jordanians?

Assistant: Shows the available Kuwait visa types for applicants from Jordan.

### Scenario 2: Informal Nationality Question

User: شو الفيز المتوفرة للبحرينيين؟

Assistant: Understands that the user is asking about Bahrain and lists the available visa types.

### Scenario 3: Tourist Visa Eligibility

User: I am a lawyer from Germany. Can I apply for a tourist visa?

Assistant: Checks the visible rules and asks for any missing information, such as age, if needed.

### Scenario 3B: Direct Eligibility Check

User clicks: Start Eligibility Check

Assistant: Opens a guided form where the user can choose nationality, visa type, and other required details to receive a direct preliminary eligibility result.

### Scenario 4: Occupation Question

User: What occupations are allowed for visa 16?

Assistant: Lists the allowed occupations for the selected visa and nationality.

### Scenario 5: Age Requirement

User: For tourist visa, what age is allowed?

Assistant: Asks for the applicant nationality if it is missing, then explains the age range for the tourist visa.

### Scenario 6: Relationship / Companion Question

User: Can I apply with my wife?

Assistant: Checks whether the wife or companion relationship is allowed for the selected visa and nationality.

### Scenario 7: Follow-Up Country Change

User: What visas are available for Pakistanis?

Assistant: Lists visa types for Pakistan.

User: What about Indians?

Assistant: Switches the nationality to India and lists the available visa types for Indian applicants.

## Purpose

This project aims to provide a clearer, faster, and more user-friendly way to explore Kuwait visa information through a conversational experience.
